Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information BELCHARD PROPERTY CO. ("we," "us") collects through https://belchardproperty.com (the "Website"), how we use it, and the choices you have. We serve customers in Oregon and Washington.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us in the quote request form:

  • your name, phone number, and email address (email is optional);
  • your property address, city and ZIP code;
  • your preferred contact method and any project notes you write;
  • the choices you make in the estimate calculator (lighting supplied by, linear feet, stories, decorations, seasonal removal) and the estimated price range shown to you;
  • photos of your home that you choose to upload; and
  • a reference number that we generate for your request.

Information from calls and texts: if you call or text us, we receive your phone number and what you tell us.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, and date and time. If you arrive through a link or QR code that contains a source or campaign tag, we may record that tag with your request so we know which marketing reached you.

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

We use it to:

  • respond to your request, contact you, prepare estimates and written quotes;
  • schedule, perform, invoice and follow up on services, and handle warranty or damage questions;
  • keep business, tax and legal records;
  • operate, secure and improve the Website and prevent spam or fraud; and
  • comply with law and protect our legal rights.

We may contact you by phone, text or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP to texts at any time. We do not send marketing texts without your separate consent.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with others for their advertising.

We share information only as needed with:

  • Netlify, Inc., our website host and form-processing provider. When you submit the quote form, Netlify Forms receives and stores your submission, including any photos, in our Netlify account. Netlify's own policy applies to its handling of data: https://www.netlify.com/privacy/
  • Email provider. When a request is submitted, a notification containing the request details is emailed to our business email account (currently Google Gmail).
  • Google Fonts. The Website loads fonts from Google, so your browser contacts Google servers and your IP address is visible to Google when you load the page.
  • Crew members and subcontractors who need the details (such as your address and project) to do the work, and who are expected to use it only for that purpose.
  • Professional advisers and service providers such as accountants, insurers, payment processors and software vendors, if and when we use them.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety or property.
  • A successor business, if we reorganize or sell the business (for example, if we form a new business entity). We would require the successor to honor this policy.

4. Project photos

Photos you upload are used to understand your roofline and plan the work. Please avoid including people, license plates, mail, documents or other personal items in photos. Photos may contain hidden data such as the time and location they were taken. We will not publish your photos in our marketing without your separate permission.

5. How long we keep it

We keep request information for as long as needed to respond to you, perform and support any services, and meet tax, accounting and legal requirements. When we no longer need it, we delete or anonymize it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Requests that never become customers may be deleted sooner on request.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission and access controls at our service providers. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it (we may need to keep some records for legal, tax or safety reasons); or
  • stop contacting you by text or email.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first. If you live in a state with a consumer privacy law that gives you additional rights, we will honor those rights to the extent the law applies to us. We will not treat you unfairly for exercising your rights.

8. Children

The Website is for adults who own or manage property. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Links and third parties

The Website may link to other sites or open phone and text apps. We are not responsible for those third parties' practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The updated version will be posted on the Website with a new effective date.
